Disconnecting QuickBooks Online

SWS Manager has been disconnected from QuickBooks Online, or you are looking for how to do so.

When SWS Manager is disconnected from QuickBooks Online, it can no longer read any information from QuickBooks. Nothing in QuickBooks is changed by disconnecting — SWS Manager's QuickBooks access is read-only.

How to disconnect

A QuickBooks Online administrator can disconnect SWS Manager at any time from within QuickBooks Online, using the list of connected apps in the company's settings. After disconnecting, Studs with Suds deletes the stored QuickBooks connection credentials for SWS Manager.

Reconnecting

SWS Manager is a private application used internally by Studs with Suds LLC. Only Studs with Suds can reconnect it to its own QuickBooks Online company.
